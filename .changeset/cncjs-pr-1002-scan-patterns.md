Add autolevel scan pattern and scan direction options. The scan pattern (Raster, Zigzag, Spiral In, Spiral Out) and the scan direction (Along X Axis, Along Y Axis) set the order in which probe points are visited. The grid, the point count and the probing itself are unchanged, and the defaults keep the previous row-by-row order. The probe area diagram and the 3D overlay mark the first and last point of the selected path.
